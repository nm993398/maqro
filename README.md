## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Does the app charge a fee?**
No. Maqro is free for personal use.

**Where does my information go?**
In guest mode, your information stays on your device. It lives in your browser storage. If you use a synced account, the data goes to our secure cloud database. We do not sell your personal data.

**Do I need a fast internet connection?**
No. You only need a connection during the initial download. Once installed, the app works entirely offline.

**How do I update the software?**
When a new version exists, the app notifies you. You visit the download page again to get the latest file. Installing the new file replaces your current version while keeping your data safe.

**Can I use this on a tablet?**
Yes. You install the app as a web application on compatible devices. You follow the same steps to set it up on your mobile browser.

## 📝 Usage tips

Start by entering your height, weight, and activity level in the settings tab. The app suggests your daily targets. Use the meal planner to log what you eat throughout the day. Check your progress in the journal section at the end of each week. Consistent logging helps you see patterns in your habits. Use the simple export feature if you ever need to save a copy of your records.
